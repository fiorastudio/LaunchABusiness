# 🦾 National Real Estate PSI: Master Study Guide (Comprehensive)

### 🏡 I. PROPERTY OWNERSHIP (8-10%)
1. **Real vs. Personal Property**: 
   - **Real**: Land, improvements, and rights (immobile).
   - **Personal (Chattel)**: Movable items. **Fixture**: Personal property attached to real estate (MARIA test: Method, Adaptability, Relationship, Intent, Agreement).
   - **Trade Fixtures**: Business-related items; tenant-owned, removable before lease ends.
2. **Land Characteristics**: 
   - **Physical**: Immobility, Indestructibility, Uniqueness (Non-homogeneity).
   - **Economic**: Scarcity, Improvements, Permanence, Area Preference (Situs).
3. **Legal Descriptions**: 
   - **Metes and Bounds**: Terminal points/angles (POB - Point of Beginning).
   - **Lot and Block**: Recorded plat maps (Subdivisions).
   - **Rectangular Survey**: Townships (6x6 miles), Sections (1x1 mile, 640 acres).
4. **Encumbrances**: 
   - **Liens**: Financial claims (Specific vs. General, Voluntary vs. Involuntary).
   - **Easements**: Appurtenant (runs with land) vs. Gross (utility companies). 
   - **Encroachments**: Physical intrusion (fence over line).

### ⚖️ II. LAND USE CONTROLS & REGULATIONS (5%)
5. **Government Powers (PETE)**: Police Power (Zoning), Escheat (Owner dies intestate), Taxation, Eminent Domain (Condemnation).
6. **Zoning**: Variances (exception for hardship), Non-conforming use (grandfathered), Special Use.
7. **Environmental Hazards**: Lead-Based Paint (built before 1978, 10-day disclosure), Radon, Asbestos, Mold, CERCLA (Superfund).

### 🤝 III. VALUATION & MARKET ANALYSIS (7%)
8. **Appraisal Principles**: Supply/Demand, Substitution (basis for all 3 approaches), Contribution, Regression/Progression.
9. **Approaches to Value**:
   - **Sales Comparison**: Best for residential; adjust *comparables*, never the subject.
   - **Cost Approach**: Replacement cost minus depreciation plus land value (best for libraries/churches).
   - **Income Approach**: Capitalization rate (NOI / Value = Cap Rate). Best for rentals.

### 💰 IV. FINANCING (10%)
10. **Mortgage Concepts**: Lien theory (borrower has title) vs. Title theory (lender has title).
11. **Loan Types**: FHA (insured), VA (guaranteed), Conventional.
12. **Laws**: 
    - **TILA/Regulation Z**: Disclosure of true loan costs (APR).
    - **RESPA**: Prohibits kickbacks, requires Loan Estimate (LE) and Closing Disclosure (CD).
    - **Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA)**: Prohibits discrimination in lending.

### 📜 V. GENERAL PRINCIPLES OF AGENCY (8-10%)
13. **Fiduciary Duties (OLD CAR)**: Obedience, Loyalty, Disclosure, Confidentiality, Accounting, Reasonable Care.
14. **Agency Types**: Universal, General (Property Manager), Special (Real Estate Agent).
15. **Termination**: Performance, Death of Broker/Client, Destruction of property, Agreement.

### 📑 VI. PROPERTY DISCLOSURES (6%)
16. **Material Facts**: Latent defects must be disclosed if known.
17. **Stigmatized Properties**: Requirements vary (Ghost/Suicide/Murder); usually depends on state law.

### 🖊️ VII. CONTRACTS (17-18%)
18. **Validity**: Offer/Acceptance, Consideration, Competent parties, Legal purpose.
19. **Status**: Void (missing element), Voidable (minor/duress), Unenforceable (not in writing).
20. **Statute of Frauds**: Requires real estate contracts to be in writing to be enforceable.
21. **Breach**: Specific Performance, Liquidated Damages, Rescission.

### 🗝️ VIII. LEASING & PROPERTY MANAGEMENT (3-4%)
22. **Leasehold Estates**: Estate for Years (fixed end), Periodic Tenancy (notice required), Tenancy at Will, Tenancy at Sufferance.
23. **Lease Types**: Gross (landlord pays all), Net (tenant pays taxes/insurance), Percentage (retail).

### 🏁 IX. TRANSFER OF TITLE (8%)
24. **Deeds**: 
    - **General Warranty**: Best protection for buyer.
    - **Quitclaim**: No warranties; clears clouds on title.
25. **Title Insurance**: Protects against past defects. Chain of Title (history).

### ⚖️ X. FAIR HOUSING (5-7%)
26. **Protected Classes**: Race, Color, Religion, National Origin, Sex, Familial Status, Disability.
27. **Violations**: Steering (directing away), Blockbusting (panic selling), Redlining (lender discrimination).

### 🧮 XI. REAL ESTATE CALCULATIONS (10%)
28. **Formulas**:
    - **Area**: Length x Width. 1 Acre = 43,560 sq ft.
    - **Commission**: Sales Price x Rate.
    - **Net to Seller**: Sales Price - (Commission + Closing Costs + Payoff).
    - **Prorations**: Interest, Taxes (Accrued vs. Prepaid).
