# 🦾 NJ Real Estate PSI: Master State-Specific Study Guide

### 🌳 LICENSING & REGULATION
1. NJ Real Estate License Act (Title 45, Chapter 15) - Protects the public.
2. NJREC Composition: 8 members (5 brokers, 2 public, 1 govt).
3. Penalties: $5k first, $10k subsequent offenses.
4. License Expiry: June 30th of odd years.
5. Temporary License: Available to salesperson with 3+ years exp (valid 1 yr).
6. Irrevocable Consent: Required for non-residents.
7. Record Retention: 6 years for contracts/trust funds.

### 📑 DISCLOSURES & CONTRACTS
8. CIS: Mandatory relationship disclosure; provided before financial talk.
9. Attorney Review: 3 business days to cancel.
10. Mandatory Seller Disclosure (2024): Required for all 1-4 unit residential sales.
11. Designated Agency: Codefied in 2024; separate agents for buyer/seller in same firm.
12. NJ Consumer Fraud Act: Triple damages for deception.

### 💰 FINANCE & TAXES
13. Realty Transfer Fee (RTF): Paid by seller.
14. GIT/REP: Exit tax for non-residents.
15. NJ Guaranty Fund: Max payout $25,000 per claim.
16. Trust Account: 5 business day deposit rule.
17. Rebate of Commission: Only from Broker to Buyer.

### 🌍 LAND USE
18. NJ LAD: Broadest fair housing (protects Section 8 income).
19. Mount Laurel Doctrine: Fair share of affordable housing.
20. PREDFDA: 7-day rescission for NJ common-interest communities.
21. CAFRA/Pinelands: Strict state-level eco-protections.

### 📢 ADVERTISING
22. Font Rule: Brokerage name must be larger than licensee name.
23. "Sold" Sign: Closing must have occurred.
